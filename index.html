A Fight for Life and Dignity

Indrapal Kaur is a remarkable woman whose life has been defined by courage in the face of illness. For the past two decades, she has bravely lived with Spinocerebellar Ataxia Type 1 (SCA1)—a rare, devastating neurodegenerative condition that has progressed to leave her completely bedridden.

Her parents poured their lives into caring for her. They were her rock, providing tireless love and support for two decades. But the reality of continuous, round-the-clock care for a patient of this complexity is immense. Her parents, who gave so much, are now elderly and physically unable to manage the intensive, specialized medical demands Indrapal requires. We are at a critical turning point.

Building an Unbreakable Safety Net

To ensure Indrapal’s care is never compromised, our mission is built on two essential, interconnected pillars.

🌟 Pillar 1: Specialized 24/7 Nursing Care

Securing her immediate, daily survival. Indrapal is in a dedicated care facility in Bangalore, where highly trained nurses monitor her every moment.

This covers: Facility fees, daily medications, specialized feeding, and continuous physiotherapy.

Fighting the progression of SCA1. We are raising funds to access cutting-edge medical treatments, including stem cell therapy and specialized consultations.

The impact: These treatments aim to slow symptom progression, enhance muscle function, and improve her overall quality of life.
