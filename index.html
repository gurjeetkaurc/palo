Section 1 路 Her Story & Condition

Living with SCA1 for 20 years

Indrapal Kaur is a 46-year-old woman who has been living with Spinocerebellar Ataxia Type 1 (SCA1) for the past 20 years. This rare, progressive neurodegenerative condition severely affects motor control, speech, and muscle coordination.

Over the last two decades, the condition has steadily progressed, leaving Indrapal completely bedridden. She can no longer perform basic daily tasks independently and requires continuous, round-the-clock nursing assistance, specialized feeding and nutrition, and regular physiotherapy to prevent complications.

Her parents devotedly cared for her at home for all these years. However, they are now elderly and no longer in a physical state to lift, move, or manage the intensive daily care she requires.

Section 3 路 Why Independent, Long-Term Support Matters

A safety net that lasts

Caring for a completely bedridden SCA1 patient is a lifelong commitment. With her aging parents unable to continue physical caregiving, and while those close to her are doing everything they can to support her today, our goal is to build an independent medical safety net so that Indrapal's nursing care and treatments remain secure for years to come, regardless of any unforeseen life changes.
